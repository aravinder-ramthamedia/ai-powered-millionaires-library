Data-Mine Yourself

How to Use AI to Turn Your Knowledge, Experience and Ideas into Products People Want, and Test Them Before You Build · by A. Ravinder · AI Powered Business Library for Future Millionaires, Book 4

What this page is for

The book shows you how to use AI as a helper to find what you already know, put it into clear words, and test whether real people need it before you build anything. Those methods outlast any one tool. Tools, plan names and features change often, so this page carries the updates. It also lists all 20 worksheets in the book, one per chapter.

  • What problem should I solve? · The WHO, RESULT and PAIN sentence (Ch 7), The word bank of 20 complaint phrases (Ch 8)
  • Is my idea any good? · The premortem list of ten reasons (Ch 9), The idea score, with confidence and lines drawn first (Ch 10)
  • Do real people want it? · Notes from ten honest conversations (Ch 11), The one-page promise (Ch 12), The real-yes count (Ch 13)
  • How do I build it? · Hand-service notes (Ch 14), The product menu (Ch 15), The weekly build rhythm (Ch 16), Value questions (Ch 17)
  • What comes next? · The first-20 list (Ch 18), The results review (Ch 19), The decision page (Ch 20)

Tools used in the book

  • ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot · general AI assistants, with Projects to keep one piece of work together
  • Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) · answers only from the files you add · notebook.google
  • Perplexity · AI search that shows its sources
  • Google Trends · how often people search for a topic over time
  • Google Forms and Tally · simple forms for sign-ups and questions
  • Carrd and Canva · a simple one-page site and simple designs
  • Calendly · lets people pick a time to talk
  • Otter · records and writes out a conversation; always ask the other person first
