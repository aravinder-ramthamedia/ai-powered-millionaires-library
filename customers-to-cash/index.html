What this page is for

The book shows you how to get found, turn leads into customers, follow up without being pushy, and get paid on time, with AI as your helper. One rule runs through it: AI drafts, you approve. Those methods outlast any one tool. Tools, plan names and features change often, so this page carries the updates. It also lists all 20 worksheets in the book, one per chapter.

Tools used in the book

  • ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot · general AI assistants that draft messages, quotes and replies for you to check
  • Google Business Profile · your business listing on Google Search and Maps
  • WhatsApp Business · a chat app for businesses, with labels and quick replies
  • Gmail and Outlook · email, with labels, filters and folders
  • Google Sheets and Excel · spreadsheets for your lead sheet, pipeline and money dashboard
  • Google Forms · a simple form builder for enquiries
  • Canva · simple visuals from one template
  • Calendly · lets people pick a time to talk
  • Trello and HubSpot · show customers as cards on a board
  • Invoicing tools such as Zoho, Wave, Stripe, PayPal and Square · make and send invoices and take payments; which ones work where you live differs
