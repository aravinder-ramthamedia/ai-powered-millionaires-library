What this page is for

The book shows you how to ask AI clearly, check its answers, use it for everyday problems and creative work, and then let an AI agent handle repeat jobs on your own computer while you stay in charge. Those methods outlast any one tool. Tools, plan names and features change often, so this page carries the updates. It also lists the chapters and the 17 workbook sheets.
